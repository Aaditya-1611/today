About the House

Darkness, Designed

NOIR is a fictional maison created as a design exercise: a study of how black, red, gold and silver can be composed into packaging that feels cinematic, weighty and luxurious.

Each of the eight concept identities explores a different material language: absorbed light, volcanic glass, polished stone, molten glow, mirrored metal. Every shape you see is pure CSS, with no frameworks; the logo is the only image.
